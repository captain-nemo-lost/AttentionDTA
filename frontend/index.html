⚖️ Generalization & Cold-Start Robustness on Davis Kinase Dataset

Comparing our reproduced AttentionDTA implementation on the Davis dataset with published results, highlighting the steep drop under cold-start conditions.

Evaluation Condition Source MSE (↓) MAE (↓) R² Score (↑) Notes / Significance

🔑 Key Scientific Takeaways

  • S1 Reproduction Confirmed: Our reproduced model achieves an average $R^2 \approx 0.6853$ on Davis, directionally consistent with the published $0.7409$.
  • The Cold-Start Collapse: When tested on 14 completely unseen drugs (drug-disjoint split), $R^2$ collapses to -0.0079.
  • Why This Matters: Deep attention models often overfit to chemical scaffold identities in the training set rather than learning universal physical binding dynamics.
